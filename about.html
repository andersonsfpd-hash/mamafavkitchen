Our Story

Nearly two decades of feeding Guelph, one plate at a time.

Home cooking, since 2006

Mama Favourite Kitchen started with a simple idea: cook food the way it's cooked at home, and share it with the neighbourhood. Nearly twenty years later, that's still exactly what happens in this kitchen every day.

African and Caribbean cooking share a lot in common — patience, bold seasoning, and a plate that's meant to be shared. Here, both traditions sit side by side on the same menu: jerk chicken off the grill, oxtail simmered for hours, curry goat, and platters of injera with stews on the side.
